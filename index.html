HOW TO PLAY — P&G GLOBAL

Core Loop

  1. Manage your Roster — Edit names, alignment (heel/face), archetypes, and personal legend icons.
  2. Book a Show — Create exactly 6 segments using different match types and spots (promo, weapons, etc.).
  3. Broadcast — Watch the show play out. Ratings, money, hype, title changes, and random live events happen.
  4. Repeat — Use what you learned to improve future bookings.

Key Systems

  • Creative HQ: Write or generate promos that actually affect wrestler popularity and story momentum.
  • Titles: World, Tag, Trios and more. Each multi-person title gives wrestlers their own physical belt.
  • Live Events: Weather, backstage attacks, and surprise moments can cancel matches or add chaos.
  • Venues: Different locations have different weather risk levels that affect your shows.

Accessibility

Open Accessibility Options from the main menu to increase text size, enable high contrast, reduce motion, etc.
